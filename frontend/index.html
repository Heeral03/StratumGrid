Core System Design

Algorithmic Complexity & Architecture

How FleetScale handles multi-robot locks, heartbeats, and safe crash recovery.

Traditional $O(N)$ Approach

Linear Subtree Traversal

Standard warehouse execution systems iterate over all sub-nodes in a zone or aisle ($O(N)$ time) before granting spatial access.

  • High contention delays during multi-robot dispatch
  • Risk of race conditions under concurrent requests
  • Stale locks remain held indefinitely if a bot crashes
FleetScale $O(h)$ Approach

Hierarchical Counter Propagation & TTL Leases

Every tree node stores parent pointers, descendant counters, and an atomic TTL lease timestamp (`expires_at`). Heartbeats renew active leases; missed heartbeats automatically trigger lock expiration.

  • Constant validation time bound by tree depth $O(h)$
  • Thread-safe mutex isolation preventing race conditions
  • Automatic crash recovery via 30s lease expiration
